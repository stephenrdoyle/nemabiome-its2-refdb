Francis & Šlapeta ITS2 reruns

Francis & Šlapeta (2023, Parasitology 150:672–682, 10.1017/S0031182023000380): 30 ITS2 runs of larval cultures from 10 cattle, 10 feral goats and 10 sheep grazing one New South Wales property (PRJNA936713). Sequenced 2x251, trimmed with bin/trim_primers.py, denoised once with DADA2 and joined three ways, plus the same reads cut to 2x150. Classified against this database (nemits2 v0.1.1 with the 2026-11-11 curation pass) and the nemabiome backbone.

  • The gap rule names 93.8–94.7% of reads to species against 70–76% for the published method (assignTaxonomy on nemabiome). Nearly all of the gain is Haemonchus: nemabiome leaves 22–29% of reads at genus, this database splits them into H. contortus and H. placei.
  • No classifier pair disagrees at species on any set: where two both name a species it is the same one.
  • Every pair here overlaps, so the three 2x250 options keep the same templates: merging loses 4.6% of reads to mismatches in the overlap, which concatenation and hybrid keep. The gap rule names 94.7% merged, 93.8% hybrid and 92.6% concatenated (the N run hides bases that separate Oesophagostomum species); the k-mer classifiers on 28S-cut ASVs stay at 99–100% whichever way.
  • 2x150 costs the gap rule nothing (94.5%).
